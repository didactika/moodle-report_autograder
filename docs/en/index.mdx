The Autograder report (`report_autograder`) is the companion of [Autograder](https://github.com/didactika/moodle-local_autograder) (`local_autograder`), the plugin that grades automatically the completed activities that no teacher has graded. Autograder does the grading; this report is where teachers and administrators review what it decided and follow the progress of each student.

<Screenshot src="asset:rp-course.webp" alt="The Autograder report of a course, with one row per student and activity, and its status, date, grade and grader" caption="The report of a course: one row per student and activity." url="/report/autograder/index.php" />

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This report requires [Autograder](https://github.com/didactika/moodle-local_autograder) (`local_autograder`) to be installed. It reads the records of Autograder and does not grade anything itself.

## What it shows

For every student of every activity where Autograder is enabled, the report shows:

- **Status:** where the student stands.
- **Date:** when the student will be graded, or when they were, and what the date is based on: the due date, an override or the moment the student finished.
- **Grade:** the grade that the student will receive, or the one that is in the gradebook now.
- **Grader:** the teacher the grade is attributed to or, for a pending student, the teacher it would be attributed to if it were given now.
