title: Settings
summary: Where the report is configured.
order: 42
---

The report has no settings of its own. Its [guided tour](page:guides/guided-tour) is managed in **Site administration > Appearance > User tours**, like every tour of Moodle.

Everything else that decides what the report shows is set in Autograder and in the capabilities of the report (see [Capabilities](page:reference/capabilities)).
