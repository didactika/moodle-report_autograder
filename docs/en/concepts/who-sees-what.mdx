The report has three levels, and each one has its own capability and its own place in Moodle.

| Report | Where to find it | Capability | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Activity | The menu of the activity | `report/autograder:view` | A teacher following the students of one activity |
| Course | The **Reports** menu of the course | `report/autograder:viewcourse` | A teacher or a course manager reviewing every activity of a course |
| Site | **Site administration > Reports > Autograder report > Grading report** | `report/autograder:viewsite` | An administrator checking that nothing is stuck |

The activity and course reports only appear where Autograder is enabled in the activity, or in at least one activity of the course.

## Groups

In an activity with separate groups, teachers see only the students of their own groups, exactly as they would in the activity itself.

## Failures

Failed gradings, and the reason for each, are only shown to the users who have `report/autograder:viewfailed`, which only managers have by default. The other users see those students as **Not autograded**.

The page **Graders by course** needs `report/autograder:viewsite` and, in the course it shows, `report/autograder:viewcourse`.

## What the report does not do

The report only reads. It does not grade, cancel or change a decision, and it stores no personal data of its own.
