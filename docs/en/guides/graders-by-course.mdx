title: Graders by course
summary: See which teacher would grade each student of a course before any grade is due.
order: 60
---

Go to **Site administration > Reports > Autograder report > Graders by course**. You need the capability `report/autograder:viewsite`, and `report/autograder:viewcourse` in the course you choose.

<Screenshot src="asset:rp-graders.webp" alt="The Graders by course page, with the teachers who could grade a course and the fallback grader" caption="The teachers who could grade a course." url="/report/autograder/graders.php" />

Choose a course. The page shows how many teachers could grade there and how many students could be graded, and lists the teachers; the fallback grader of the site appears with a label. Select **See which grader each student would get** to list the students with the teacher each would be graded as.

It is useful to check a course before any grade is due. A course where nobody can grade is flagged, since every grade in it would fail unless a fallback grader is configured in Autograder.

The page works from the course. The report of an activity checks the permissions and the groups of that activity before showing the grader that actually applies.
