The first time a person opens the report of an activity or of a course, a guided tour presents it in seven steps: what the report shows, the search, the **Status** menu, the **Grading date** menu, the button that removes every filter, the columns of the table and the pages of the list. Each step points to the part of the page it explains.

<Screenshot src="asset:rp-tour.webp" alt="A step of the guided tour of the report, pointing to the Status menu" caption="A step of the tour, beside the part of the page it explains." url="/report/autograder/index.php" />

The tour is in the language of the person who reads it, in English, Spanish, French, Italian and Portuguese. Select **Next** to go on; on the last step, **End tour** closes it.

## See it again

The tour appears once for each person. To see it again, select the link that resets the user tour of the page, at the foot of the report.

## Required or optional

By default the tour can be skipped at any step with **Skip tour**. An administrator can make it required with the setting **Require the guided tour** (see [Settings](page:reference/settings)): every step then has only **Next**, and the tour ends on its last step. The Escape key still closes it, as in every tour of Moodle.

## Change the tour

The tour is a Moodle user tour named **Autograder report**. An administrator can change its steps, its texts or the people who see it in **Site administration > Appearance > User tours**. Its texts are language strings of the report, which can also be changed in the language customisation of the site.
