Open the activity and select **Autograder report** in its menu. You need the capability `report/autograder:view` in the activity.

<Screenshot src="asset:rp-activity.webp" alt="The Autograder report of an assignment, with one row per student" caption="One row per student of the activity." url="/report/autograder/index.php" />

The table lists every student of the activity with their groups, status, grading date, grade and grader. The first time you open the report, a [guided tour](page:guides/guided-tour) presents each part of it.

## Narrow the list

The row above the table holds the search and one menu for each filter:

- the **search box** looks for a student by name as you type;
- **Status** lists every status with the number of students in it; tick one or more to see only those students;
- the group menu, which reads **All groups** until a group is chosen, narrows the list to one group, and only appears when the activity uses groups;
- **Grading date** limits the list to a period: today, the last seven days, the current month, the last thirty days or two dates of your choice.

A menu that narrows the list is highlighted and shows only what it is narrowed to: for example **Pending**, or **Status: 2** when two statuses are ticked. The cross that replaces its arrow removes that filter. The button with the reload icon at the end of the row removes the search and every filter at once.

The row of filters never breaks into two lines. When the menus do not fit beside the search, for example on a phone, they are gathered in one menu, **Filters**, with the funnel icon, which shows how many filters are in use and holds every menu inside it. A long value is cut with an ellipsis before the cross of its menu.

<Screenshot src="asset:rp-filters.webp" alt="The Status menu of the report open, with the number of students in each status" caption="Each status shows how many students it has inside the other filters." url="/report/autograder/index.php" />

## Read and move through the list

Select the heading of a sortable column to sort by it, and select it again to reverse the order. The table keeps the same height whatever it holds: when it has more rows than fit, it scrolls inside its box and the headings stay in view.

Under the table, **Results per page** offers the sizes smaller than the list, and **All**: a list of up to 12 students shows all of them; one of 13 to 24 offers 12 or all; and so on up to 12, 24, 48 and 96. The arrows move to the previous and the next page.

The report only appears in the menu of the activity when Autograder is enabled in it. For the meaning of each column, see [Statuses, dates and graders](page:concepts/statuses-and-dates).
